How EazyCV works

Go from blank page to a polished CV in three simple steps.

1. Tell us about yourself

Fill in your personal details, education, skills, and experience in the guided form. No complex fields — just clear prompts.

2. Preview your CV

We convert your answers into a clean CV layout instantly so you can see exactly what recruiters will see.

3. Refine with AI

Use the optional "Optimize with AI" button to turn your summary into a sharper, more impactful profile statement.
